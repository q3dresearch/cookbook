# Asked, answered, open

## Answered

**Can this register measure survival at all?** Not directly — `DissolutionDate` is never
populated and every status present is one a live company holds. It becomes measurable only
because the publisher keeps dated monthly files that nothing links to, so disappearance
between snapshots is the death definition.

**Does it agree with the official series?** On ordering, yes: rho = +0.79 across 14 sectors.
On level, no: the register is below ONS in 11 of 14. Different populations, different deaths,
different periods.

**Is the strike-off warning informative?** Only while it stands. Companies that survive a
proposal to strike off return to an ordinary company's hazard, so a withdrawn warning carries
no residual signal.

**Are formation-agent addresses different?** Yes, and not only because their companies are
newer: two-year survival 37% against 56%, with 8.9 points surviving age standardisation. In
2012 the two groups were indistinguishable — whatever changed, changed recently.

**Does place matter beyond industry mix?** Yes in a few places. Dudley's excess survives
holding industry, age and size constant.

## Open

**What happens to a company after it disappears?** Unknown here. Dissolution, strike-off and
acquisition all look identical — an absence. Companies House publishes a separate filing
history product that would distinguish them.

*What would close it:* joining the filing-history product on company number for a sample of
disappearances. The join key is certain; the cost is the volume.

**Is the agent-address effect selection or treatment?** Not answerable with a register that
carries the address and the outcome and nothing about the relationship. The honest statement
is the association plus the age standardisation, which is what the figure gives.

**Does the entry-mix shift survive cohort recency?** Not established. The confound could not
be removed with three snapshots and the figure says so rather than adjusting it away. A fourth
snapshot in 2027 would separate them.

**Why is Norway's shape different?** Measured, not explained. Comparing definitions of death
across two registers needs the statutory duty behind each, which is reading rather than data.
